
# Eric Devlin

Design technologist. I lead UX engineering at a large bank: design systems and AI tooling so product teams ship without waiting on a designer. Before software, eleven years in fashion, owning handbag and shoe lines from concept to market. Product management, in leather.

## Current

**[Lookout](https://github.com/ericseandevlin/lookout)**: a personal search agent. You tell it what you want, it searches twice a week, reads every posting and puts the ones that fit on your board with a score and the reasons. Live at [lookout.ericdevlin.co](https://lookout.ericdevlin.co). Next.js, MIT.

**[Taste Engine](https://github.com/ericseandevlin/taste-engine)**: teach an AI a specific visual taste from a board of references, then make it prove it. Taste as version-controlled data with forbidden moves, not vibes in a prompt. Gemini/OpenRouter, runs locally, MIT.
